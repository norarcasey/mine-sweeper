# Nora's Mine Sweeper: privacy policy

_Last updated 6 November 2026._

Nora's Mine Sweeper is a Minesweeper game for iPhone, made by Nora Casey.

**The app collects no data.** It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking,
and it never connects to the internet. Nothing you do in it leaves your phone.

It stores two things: the difficulty you last picked, so the next game starts at the same level,
and your best time on each level. Both stay on your phone, in the app's own storage, and are
deleted when you delete the app.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.

Questions: see the [support page](support.md).
