# Nora's Mine Sweeper: support

Nora's Mine Sweeper is a Minesweeper game for iPhone, made by Nora Casey.

## Get help or report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/norarcasey/mine-sweeper/issues/new) on this project's GitHub
page, and say which iPhone and which iOS version you have.

## How to play

- **Tap** a square to open it. Your first tap is always safe.
- A number says how many of the eight squares around it hold a mine.
- **Long-press** a square to plant a flag on a mine, or turn on **Flag** so a tap flags instead.
- **Tap a number** whose flags match it to open the rest of its neighbours.
- You win by opening every safe square, or by flagging every mine and nothing else.
- The **face** starts a new game. Leaving the app pauses the game, and so does **Pause**.

## Privacy

The app collects nothing. See the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
